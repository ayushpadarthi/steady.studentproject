How it works. Normal stabilisers average your recent movement, so the line trails behind the pen. Steady tells apart the slow movement you mean from the fast back-and-forth of a tremor, removes only the shake, and mostly stays out of the way when there is none.

Want to see it? Open Draw, choose Demo, and turn on a simulated tremor.

A student science fair project. Not a medical device. Nothing you draw leaves your device.
