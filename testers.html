Steady, for testers

A checklist for trying Steady on a real device and stylus. Mark each step, add a note when something is off, then download the report and give it to the team.

How to use this page. Open the linked page in another tab, do the step, come back and press Works, Problem or Skip. A note box opens for problems: say what you did and what you saw. Your marks are saved on this device only.

This is for testing the app (bugs, layout, feel). It is not the study with participants, and it records no drawings.

Before you start

  • Use the device you want to test: laptop, Chromebook, iPad, Android tablet or phone. Note the stylus, if any.
  • Browser zoom at 100 %. Chrome or Edge are needed only for the tremor pen.
  • Have any bank, library or school ID card ready for "Set real size".

Hardware testers: the tremor pen

The tremor pen is a servo that shakes a stylus repeatably, with a motion sensor that measures the real shake. It is test equipment, not part of the app. Its full bench test is in the team's hardware folder (hardware/docs/BENCH_TEST.md). In short:

  1. Flash the firmware, open a serial monitor at 921600 baud, type Z (centre) and T,6,1.5,0 (shake at 6 Hz, 1.5 mm). X stops it; it also stops itself after 15 s.
  2. In the Lab (Chrome or Edge): set real size, enter the pen's arm length, Connect, tick Shake the real pen, draw a slow line with the filter set to raw. It should wiggle by at least 70 % of the asked size at 4-6 Hz.
  3. With the motion sensor fitted: draw with Steady on for 3 seconds, press Record this row, repeat over 4-8 Hz and 1-3 mm, download the CSV.
A student science fair project. Not a medical device. Nothing you draw leaves your device.
