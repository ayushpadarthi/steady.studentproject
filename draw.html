Smoothing

While you draw, Steady keeps the line fast. When you lift the pen, the stroke can settle into a smoother line. The amount follows the shake Steady has measured.

Shake style experimental

Steady separates your shake from the line you meant. Instead of only deleting it, it can become part of the brush. The line still goes where you meant it to.

Turn Steady on to use a shake style.
