All three boxes draw the same movement at the same moment. The small ring is where your pen really is. Shakiness: how far the line wanders from the path you meant (mm). Behind your pen: how far the line trails the pen while you move (mm). When you lift the pen, Steady tidies the stroke it just drew, as the drawing app does. The shake is simulated from published tremor data; a real hand with tremor can use "No tremor added".
